Questions

Is this the official SAT?

No. These are original SAT-style questions written by our team. They follow the content areas of the digital SAT. We are not affiliated with College Board.

Will I get an SAT score?

Yes, an approximate score range for each section on the 200–800 scale, clearly marked as approximate, because 25 questions give only a rough estimate. You'll also see how many you got right in each topic, which is what you need to decide what to study.

How long does it take?

About 35 minutes: 14 minutes for Reading & Writing and 21 minutes for Math.

Can I use a calculator?

Yes. A scientific calculator and a reference sheet are built into the Math section.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You don't need an account or an email address. Your answers stay in your browser.

Can I take it again?

Yes. Keep in mind the questions are the same, so a second attempt mostly measures what you remember from the explanations.
