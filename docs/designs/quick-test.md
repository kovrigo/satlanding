- Время: 3 минуты на чтение, 2 минуты на математику, всего 5 минут.
- Все вопросы лёгкие: короткий текст, один шаг решения, примерно 30 секунд на вопрос.
- Темы покрывают все разделы SAT: 4 темы по чтению и 4 по математике.
- Один вопрос по математике без вариантов: ответ вводится числом, как на настоящем SAT.

## Reading & Writing

### 1. Words in Context (Craft & Structure)

> Maya was ______ to hear that she had won the school art contest: she jumped up and hugged her teacher.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word?

- A) bored
- B) confused
- **C) thrilled**
- D) annoyed

Explanation: Jumping up and hugging the teacher shows great joy. Only “thrilled” matches that reaction.

### 2. Main idea (Information & Ideas)

> Honeybees communicate through movement. When a bee finds a good patch of flowers, it returns to the hive and performs a “waggle dance.” The direction and length of the dance tell the other bees where the flowers are.

Which choice best states the main idea of the text?

- A) Honeybees prefer flowers that grow close to the hive.
- **B) Honeybees use a dance to tell each other where food is.**
- C) Honeybees cannot see the colors of flowers.
- D) Only the oldest bees in a hive are able to dance.

Explanation: The whole text describes how the dance shows other bees where the flowers are. The other choices are never mentioned.

### 3. Data in a table (Information & Ideas)

> Visitors to the city zoo: 2021 — 40,000; 2022 — 55,000; 2023 — 70,000.
>
> According to the table, from 2021 to 2023 the number of visitors to the zoo ______

On the site the numbers appear as a small table. Which choice most effectively uses data from the table to complete the statement?

- A) decreased every year.
- B) stayed the same every year.
- C) was highest in 2021.
- **D) increased every year.**

Explanation: The number grows from 40,000 to 55,000 to 70,000, so it went up each year.

### 4. Grammar (Standard English Conventions)

> Every morning, my grandmother ______ a cup of green tea in her garden.

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

- A) drink
- **B) drinks**
- C) are drinking
- D) have drunk

Explanation: The subject “grandmother” is singular, and “every morning” describes a habit. The singular present form is “drinks.”

### 5. Transitions (Expression of Ideas)

> Kai practiced the piano for an hour every day. ______ he played his piece perfectly at the school concert.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

- **A) As a result,**
- B) However,
- C) For example,
- D) In contrast,

Explanation: Daily practice led to a perfect performance. That is cause and effect: “As a result.”

## Math

### 6. Linear equation (Algebra)

If 2x + 7 = 19, what is the value of x?

- A) 4
- **B) 6**
- C) 12
- D) 13

Explanation: Subtract 7 from both sides: 2x = 12. Divide by 2: x = 6.

### 7. Percent (Problem-Solving & Data Analysis)

A shirt costs $40. It is on sale for 25% off. What is the sale price?

- A) $10
- B) $25
- **C) $30**
- D) $35

Explanation: 25% of $40 is $10. The sale price is $40 − $10 = $30. Choice A is the discount, not the price.

### 8. Function value (Advanced Math)

The function f is defined by f(x) = x² + 1. What is the value of f(3)?

- A) 6
- B) 7
- C) 9
- **D) 10**

Explanation: Substitute 3: 3² + 1 = 9 + 1 = 10.

### 9. Area (Geometry & Trigonometry)

A rectangle has a length of 8 centimeters and a width of 5 centimeters. What is the area of the rectangle, in square centimeters?

- **A) 40**
- B) 13
- C) 26
- D) 80

Explanation: Area is length times width: 8 × 5 = 40. Choice C is the perimeter.

### 10. Proportion, typed answer (Problem-Solving & Data Analysis)

A recipe uses 3 cups of flour to make 12 cookies. How many cups of flour are needed to make 36 cookies?

The student types the number. Correct answer: **9**.

Explanation: 36 cookies is 3 times 12 cookies, so the recipe needs 3 times the flour: 3 × 3 = 9 cups.
