# Tutorial: take the starter diagnostic

You open the landing, take the 25-question test and read your result.

1. Open the site's front page `/`, or the one-file site from disk (see «How to send the site as one file»). Scroll to «Try one now» and pick an answer: the page says if it is right.
2. Press «Start the free test». The start screen shows two sections: Reading and Writing, 12 questions, 14 minutes; Math, 13 questions, 21 minutes.
3. Press Start. Answer a question, press Next. Press the flag to mark a question for review.
4. Open the question navigator at the bottom to jump to any question. When done, open the review page and press Submit.
5. The break screen opens. Start Math. Use the calculator and the reference sheet from the toolbar.
6. Submit Math. The result page shows score ranges, topics, weak spots and every question explained.
7. Fill in the waitlist form. Your mail app opens a letter to hello@bagira.study with your result.
